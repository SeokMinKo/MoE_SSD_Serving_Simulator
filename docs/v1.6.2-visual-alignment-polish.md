# V1.6.2 Visual Alignment Polish

## Scope

Scenario controls and shared interactive geometry only. Simulation semantics, copy, chart data, and workflow behavior are out of scope.

## Baseline evidence

Candidate: public Pages release `c2d17137f862e79f6083b3ff903217e9cf552cb6`.

| Area | Weight | Baseline | Finding |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Help glyph containment and centering | 30 | 14 | Desktop `.helpTip` is 18×18px but inherits 8px button padding; its 20px scroll height exceeds the control and the `?` visibly escapes the circle. |
| Form-label and control alignment | 25 | 20 | Labels align consistently, but mobile uses the complete 44px help button as the visible decoration, producing an oversized rounded control beside compact labels. |
| Responsive clipping and overflow | 20 | 20 | CDP geometry at 375×812 and 1440×900 reports no document or visible-control overflow. |
| Light/Dark consistency | 15 | 14 | The defect reproduces from shared geometry in both themes; palette and contrast remain coherent. |
| Accessibility and interaction | 10 | 9 | Help controls retain accessible labels and mobile 44px hit targets, but visual containment is broken on desktop. |
| **Total** | **100** | **77** | Not release-grade for alignment polish. |

## Target contract

- The interactive hit target and visible help circle are separate layers.
- The visible circle is 20×20px with a centered `?`, explicit zero internal padding, line-height 1, and clipped glyph bounds.
- Desktop hit targets are at least 28×28px; mobile hit targets remain 44×44px.
- No visible help glyph exceeds its circle in either theme.
- No document-level horizontal overflow or visible control clipping at 375×812 and 1440×900.
- Existing help focus, click, popover, and accessible-name behavior remains unchanged.

## Release gates

- Focused RED→GREEN contract test.
- `npm run check`, full `npm test`, audit, release build, and `git diff --check`.
- Fresh production screenshots for 1440×900 and 375×812 in Light and Dark themes.
- Runtime geometry proof and console/error inspection.
- Fresh independent visual review with blocker 0 and design score at least 95/100.
